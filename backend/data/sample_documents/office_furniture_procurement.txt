PROCUREMENT SPECIFICATION
=========================

Organization: Government Secretariat — General Services Division
Document No: GSD/PROC/2025/FUR/018
Date: 01-Aug-2025

Subject: Procurement of Office Furniture for New Administrative Block

1. SCOPE
--------
Supply, delivery and installation of modular office furniture for the
newly constructed administrative block. Items include office tables,
chairs, filing cabinets, and storage units.

2. ITEMS REQUIRED
------------------
2.1 Steel Office Tables (Executive) — 50 nos.
2.2 Steel Office Tables (Workstation) — 200 nos.
2.3 Revolving Office Chairs (with armrest) — 250 nos.
2.4 Visitor Chairs (steel frame) — 150 nos.
2.5 4-Drawer Filing Cabinets — 100 nos.
2.6 Steel Almirahs (full height) — 75 nos.

3. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------
3.1 All steel furniture must conform to relevant BIS standards.
3.2 Minimum 20-gauge CRCA sheet for body panels.
3.3 Powder-coated finish in approved shade.
3.4 Edge-banding on all exposed edges.
3.5 Anti-tilt mechanism on filing cabinets.
3.6 Adjustable seat height for chairs (400mm to 520mm).
3.7 Five-star base with castor wheels for revolving chairs.

4. MATERIAL REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------
4.1 Body: Cold Rolled Close Annealed (CRCA) steel
4.2 Tabletop: 25mm pre-laminated particle board
4.3 Chair upholstery: High-density foam with fabric

5. STANDARDS COMPLIANCE
------------------------
5.1 Tables must conform to IS 4847.
5.2 Chairs must conform to IS 5765.
5.3 Filing cabinets and storage units must conform to IS 6665.

6. WARRANTY
-----------
Minimum 3 years against manufacturing defects.

PROCUREMENT SPECIFICATION
=========================

Organization: Municipal Corporation - Smart City Division
Document No: PROC/2025/LED/0042
Date: 15-Sep-2025

Subject: Procurement of Outdoor LED Street Lighting Systems

1. SCOPE
--------
This specification covers the procurement of outdoor LED street lighting
systems for installation on municipal roads and public areas under the
Smart City Mission programme.

2. QUANTITY
-----------
Total: 500 units of LED street light luminaires

3. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------
3.1 Type: LED Street Light Luminaire
3.2 Wattage: 90W to 120W
3.3 Luminous Efficacy: Minimum 120 lm/W
3.4 Colour Temperature: 4000K to 5700K (Neutral White to Cool White)
3.5 CRI (Colour Rendering Index): Minimum 70
3.6 IP Rating: Minimum IP65
3.7 Operating Voltage: 100V to 270V AC
3.8 Power Factor: Greater than 0.95
3.9 THD: Less than 15%
3.10 Rated Life: Minimum 50,000 hours
3.11 Surge Protection: Minimum 10kV

4. ENVIRONMENTAL CONDITIONS
-----------------------------
4.1 Operating Temperature: -10°C to 50°C
4.2 Humidity: Up to 95% RH
4.3 Application: Outdoor, pole-mounted

5. MATERIAL REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------
5.1 Housing: Die-cast aluminium with powder coating
5.2 Lens: Toughened glass or polycarbonate
5.3 Mounting: Universal bracket for 40mm to 60mm pole diameter

6. SAFETY & COMPLIANCE
------------------------
6.1 All luminaires must conform to relevant IS standards.
6.2 Reference standards: IS 10322, IS 16102, IS 16107.
6.3 BIS certification (ISI Mark) is mandatory.
6.4 Luminaires must have valid test reports from NABL accredited laboratory.

7. TESTING REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------
7.1 Photometric testing as per IS 16107
7.2 Safety testing as per IS 16102
7.3 IP rating testing as per IS 12063
7.4 Surge immunity testing as per IS/IEC 61547

8. WARRANTY
-----------
Minimum 5 years comprehensive warranty including driver and LED module.

9. DELIVERY
-----------
Within 90 days from date of purchase order.

PROCUREMENT SPECIFICATION
=========================

Organization: PWD Electrical Division
Document No: PWD/ELEC/2025/SW/007
Date: 20-Jul-2025

Subject: Procurement of Electrical Switches and Wiring for Government Building

1. SCOPE
--------
Supply of electrical switches, socket outlets, PVC insulated cables, and
flexible cords for a government office building renovation project.

2. ITEMS REQUIRED
------------------
2.1 Modular Switches (6A/16A) — 2000 nos.
2.2 Socket Outlets (5-pin, 16A) — 500 nos.
2.3 PVC Insulated Cables (1.5 sq mm, single core) — 5000 metres
2.4 PVC Insulated Cables (4 sq mm, single core) — 2000 metres
2.5 Flexible Cords (0.75 sq mm, 3-core) — 1000 metres

3. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------
3.1 Switches must be rated for 240V AC, 50Hz.
3.2 All switches must comply with IS 13252.
3.3 Cables must comply with IS 1554 (Part 1) — latest revision.
3.4 Flexible cords must comply with IS 694.
3.5 Flame retardant grade cables required.
3.6 Switches must have ISI Mark certification.

4. QUALITY REQUIREMENTS
-------------------------
4.1 BIS certification (ISI Mark) mandatory for all items.
4.2 Valid BIS licence number must be printed on products.
4.3 Products must have NABL-accredited test reports.

5. REFERENCE TO OLD STANDARD
------------------------------
Note: Previous order referenced IS 1554 (Part 1):1988.
Vendors should supply cables conforming to the latest revision.

6. WARRANTY
-----------
Minimum 2 years for switches, 10 years for cables.
